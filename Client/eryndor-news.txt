# Realm Dispatches

## Update - 19 September 2026

### Parties & Social
- **Parties now hold five heroes** - the cap rose from four to five, counting you
- **Leader crown and class icons** - the party window crowns the leader and shows every member's class
- **Class tooltips** - hover a party member for name, level and class; offline members show greyed names and an "- Offline" tag
- **Right-click party actions** - message, kick and promote straight from the party window
- The party HUD class badge now rides beside the level text and follows it as levels grow

### Chat
- **Two new tabs: Whisper and Trade** - every channel now has its own home
- **/w and /whisper** - send a private message to any player; /pm, /msg, /r and /reply still work
- **/trade** - a new world-wide channel for offers and requests
- WoW-style channel colours - whispers magenta, trade caramel, party green, guild orange
- A wider chatbox so all tabs and buttons fit cleanly
- Speech bubbles now anchor right above your health bars instead of floating high overhead

### Interface
- **Settings - Game - "Show my own name"** - show your own name and guild tag overhead, MMO style
- **Settings - Interface - "Show extra action bar"** - the second hotbar row is now a saved option; the + button is gone
- The warrior defensive stance button now rides the left end of the health bar
- New Archer class icon
